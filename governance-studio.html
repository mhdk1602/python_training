Why pressure fields

Institutional pressures live at multiple scales.

A regulation operates strongest at the firm scale. A peer benchmark operates strongest at the firm and division scale. A professional code operates strongest at the practitioner scale. The single-number compliance score collapses this structure.

Why drift matters

Translation compounds.

A regulation does not arrive at the practitioner intact. It cascades through firm policy, engagement SOP, and practitioner action. Each step drifts. By the time the cascade reaches the keyboard, the regulatory intent may be unrecognizable.
